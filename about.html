Why I started this

I needed a space that didn't make me choose.

For a long time, it felt like I had to pick a lane — bring my questions to a prayer group, or bring my hurt to a therapist, but never both in the same room. Faith spaces sometimes rushed past the pain to get to the promise. Wellness spaces sometimes left God at the door.

Rooted in Grace started as a handful of voice notes to a friend, and a folder of half-finished stories I was too afraid to publish. It grew into this: a sanctuary for the stories that don't have neat endings yet — where hope and honesty, faith and mental wellness, get to sit at the same table.

If you've ever felt like your questions were too messy for church and too spiritual for the clinic, this space is for you. You were never meant to walk it alone.

The mission

Six things we're building toward.

Rooted in Grace exists to hold space for the whole of a person's healing — spiritual, emotional, and communal.

01

Hope

A reminder, in seasons that feel stuck, that something can still be growing underground.

02

Healing

Space for the slow, non-linear work of getting better — without a deadline attached.

03

Encouragement

Honest words for hard days, offered gently and without a hint of judgment.

04

Faith-based reflection

Scripture and story held together — faith that makes room for real questions.

05

Mental wellness conversations

Talking openly about therapy, anxiety, and grief as part of a faithful life, not apart from it.

06

Community

People who will check in on you, pray for you, and remind you that you're not walking it alone.
