From the Sidewalk

Share your story

Ordinary streets, extraordinary grace. Every story in this series begins with a simple question: where did grace find you?

The series

Honest accounts, told your way.

From the Sidewalk is our signature story series — unpolished, real accounts of faith met in everyday places. A hospital waiting room. A parking lot after a hard phone call. A sidewalk. We publish these stories to remind people that grace shows up in ordinary places, not just tidy ones.

If a piece of your story belongs here, we'd be honored to hold it gently and help you share it — however much or little of it you're ready to tell.

Before you write

A few gentle expectations.

We built this process to feel safe, not like a submission form into the void. Here's exactly what to expect.

1

Anonymity is offered

Share under your name, a first name only, or fully anonymously. You choose, and you can change your mind at any point before publishing.

2

Editing, with your consent

We may lightly edit for length or clarity, but nothing goes live without your review and your yes. Your words, your story, your final say.

3

No perfect endings required

Stories still being lived are welcome here. You don't need a resolved, tidy ending — just an honest one.
